How It Works

Simple as sending a text

No app to learn. No forms to fill in. Just WhatsApp — the tool you already use every day on site.

Step 01
📱

Text or voice note

Send a message to SubSync on WhatsApp. Speak naturally — exactly like you'd text a mate on site.

Step 02
🧠

AI understands it

SubSync classifies your message — variation, daywork, material order — and saves it tagged to the right site.

Step 03
📄

Documents generated

Say "generate variations for Site X." Branded PDFs land in your WhatsApp in seconds — ready to send.

Step 04
✉️

Send to client

Review on your dashboard, hit send, and the PDF goes straight to your client. Done in under a minute.
